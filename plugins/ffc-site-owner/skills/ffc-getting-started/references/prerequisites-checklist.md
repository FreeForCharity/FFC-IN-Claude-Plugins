# FFC prerequisites ("Phase 0") — the ordered checklist

Source of truth: FFC's founder-approved *Application & Prerequisites* inventory and
the guides at https://ffcadmin.org/guides/. Everything here applies to **both**
tracks (501(c)(3) and pre-501(c)(3)). Work top to bottom — later steps depend on
earlier ones.

Guide links are `https://ffcadmin.org/guides/<slug>/`.

## Group A — Devices & connectivity

| Item | Requirement | Why it helps you |
|---|---|---|
| Smartphone | An iPhone or Android you own, able to install apps, **still receiving OS/security updates**. A tablet doesn't count. | It runs the authenticator apps that protect every account. |
| US mobile number | Active US cell number on that phone that can send/receive texts. | Sign-in and recovery codes; FFC can text you during setup. |
| Computer | Windows or Mac **you can install software on** (admin rights), still getting OS updates (e.g. Windows 11 or a supported macOS), **8 GB+ RAM**. | This is where you edit your site and use AI tools; some tools must be installed on a desktop. |
| Internet | Reliable broadband/Wi-Fi for both. | Everything FFC provides is cloud-based. |
| Webcam + mic | On the computer or the phone. | Live help calls with FFC over Teams. |

If they're unsure whether the computer is still supported: on Windows, Settings →
System → About shows the version and RAM; on Mac, Apple menu → About This Mac.

## Group B — Software on your devices

Do **Microsoft Teams first** — installing it proves the computer can install
software and gives you the channel FFC uses to help you live.

1. **Microsoft Teams** — desktop app on the computer **and** the phone app. Sign in
   with a free personal Microsoft account for now; your charity email is added later.
   Guide: `microsoft-teams`
2. **Browser: Chrome or Edge**, signed into your Google (Chrome) or Microsoft (Edge)
   account. Firefox and Safari aren't supported. Guides: `chrome`, `edge`
3. **Google Authenticator** and **Microsoft Authenticator** — **both** are required.
   Google's for Google-ecosystem accounts, Microsoft's for Microsoft 365. Prefer an
   authenticator app over text-message codes wherever offered.
   Guide: `multi-factor-authentication`
4. **Password manager — holistic.** At least one tied to your phone (Apple
   Passwords/iCloud Keychain on iPhone/Mac, or Google Password Manager on Android)
   **plus** your browser's built-in one. Store your MFA recovery codes there.
   A unique password for every service — never reused; email and banking most of all.
   LastPass comes later, at the organizational phase, for sharing with your team.
   Guide: `password-manager`
5. **Passkeys** — turn them on where offered (e.g. Windows Hello, your phone's
   passkey), **in addition to** the authenticator apps. Guide: `passkeys`
6. **Calendar app** (Google Calendar or Outlook) and **documents app** (Word or
   Google Docs).
7. **Cloud storage + scanning** — Google Drive or OneDrive, and a way to scan
   documents (a phone scanning app is fine). Scan the IRS letter and formation papers
   into it. Guide: `cloud-storage-scanning`

## Group C — Your email & recovery

- A **primary personal email** you check, on your phone (Android → Gmail; iPhone →
  iCloud or Gmail).
- A **secondary recovery email** or a trusted backup person's address, so a lost
  phone never means permanent lockout.

## Group D — Your personal accounts

Each is **you, in your real name**, with MFA turned on where offered. The
organization's presence is built on top of these later.

| Account | Notes | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| GitHub | Real-name username (e.g. `JaneSmith`). This is how you'll edit and approve changes to your website. Add your charity email to it later — never a separate "charity" login. | `github-account` |
| LinkedIn (personal) | Real name, photo, headline. Needed to create the organization's Page. **Every board / planned-board member needs one too.** | `linkedin` |
| Facebook (personal) | Real name. Needed to create the organization's Page. Never a fake "charity person" account — Facebook removes those. | `facebook` |
| Canva | You'll join the charity's Canva team later. | `canva` |
| Idealist | Experience it as a volunteer first. (VolunteerMatch is now part of Idealist.) | `idealist` |
| Candid | The nonprofit registry; use it to find comparable charities (Group F). | `candid` |
| Taproot | Skills-based / pro-bono volunteering marketplace. | `taproot` |

## Group E — Skills, AI & time

- **Basic digital literacy** — opening sites, filling forms, scanning a QR code,
  installing an app.
- **Willingness to use AI tools.** FFC's standard pairing for your *first*
  assistant matches your main email: Gmail/iCloud → **Google Gemini**;
  Outlook.com → **Microsoft Copilot**. **Claude** is the recommended next tool —
  and it's what FFC's website-editing flow uses. Use personal AI plans first;
  organizational AI subscriptions only make sense once the charity is much larger.
  Guide: `ai-assistant`
- **Dedicated time** — a realistic block to finish this list, plus ongoing time to
  keep the site current.

## Group F — Organization basics

- **Settled organization name**, and check whether the matching `.org` is available
  using the domain search at cloudflare.com. **Don't buy it** — FFC registers the
  domain later, after your website is validated.
- **Drafted mission statement** — short; you'll reuse it on LinkedIn, Facebook,
  Candid, and your site. Offer to help them tighten their own draft; it should be
  their words.
- **Three comparable charities** with the same or nearly the same mission, ideally
  nearby, found via Candid and Idealist. Note what makes yours distinct.
- **Public organization phone number** — a free Google Voice number works well
  (guide: `google-voice`); a personal cell is fine if they're comfortable being
  publicly contacted.
- **US mailing address you accept will be public** — a PO box or home address is
  fine, but it will be associated with the nonprofit publicly.

## Group G — Governance & agreement

- **At least two accountable people** — you plus a backup admin (usually a board
  member), so nothing dies with one login.
- **Read and accept the FFC Terms of Service** (Section 18 — Supported Charities):
  https://ffcadmin.org/terms-of-service/ — this includes the **full-stack
  commitment** (use the whole FFC stack, not pick-and-choose), conflict-of-interest
  and data-handling terms, using AI responsibly, and **crediting FFC**.

## The organization Pages (the litmus test — required before applying)

**LinkedIn organization Page** (guide: `linkedin`)
1. From your personal profile: For Business → Create a Company Page → Company /
   Nonprofit. Name, website (if any), logo.
2. Add at least one other admin; complete About; add logo + banner; publish a first
   post.
3. **Each board / planned-board member** adds the charity under the **Volunteering**
   section of their personal LinkedIn profile, pointing to the Page. This signals
   they've accepted the position and its responsibility.

**Facebook organization Page** (guide: `facebook`)
1. From your personal account: Create → Page, category **Nonprofit Organization**,
   short description, logo as profile picture, banner as cover.
2. Add at least one more admin (Settings → Page access) and publish a welcome post.
