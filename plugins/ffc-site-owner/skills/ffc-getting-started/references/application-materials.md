# Applying to FFC — materials, submission, and what happens next

## Where to apply

Apply through the **FFC client portal**: https://freeforcharity.org/hub
Choose the application product for your track — **501(c)(3)** or **pre-501(c)(3)**.

The informational pages that explain each track:
- 501(c)(3): https://freeforcharity.org/501c3/
- Pre-501(c)(3): https://freeforcharity.org/pre501c3/
- Recommended tools and setup order: https://freeforcharity.org/free-for-charitys-tools-for-success/

The application is the only front door. A GitHub issue is *not* an application —
FFC creates one later as the work order to build the website.

## The application asks for

- Your organization's **Facebook Page** and **LinkedIn Page** URLs (required)
- A **Terms of Service certification** (https://ffcadmin.org/terms-of-service/,
  Section 18)
- A **US-based attestation**
- Questions about how you use **AI**
- **Board contacts** — President/Chair, Secretary, Treasurer (optionally Vice
  Chair, Member-at-Large). Pre-501(c)(3): your **planned** board.
- **Primary and technical contacts**, with time zone and best contact hours
- (501(c)(3) track) your **Candid Public Profile and Full Profile links**

The details you enter are used to build your website's footer automatically, so get
the legal name, EIN, and social links exactly right.

## Materials to have ready

"Missing prerequisites are the single biggest source of stuck onboardings" — gather
these before starting the form, ideally scanned into your cloud storage.

**Both tracks**
- EIN (Employer Identification Number)
- Your personal LinkedIn profile URL
- Board members: names, titles, contact info, personal LinkedIn URLs, short bios
- Organization Facebook and LinkedIn Page URLs
- Mission statement
- List of programs and services
- Current operating budget
- Current website URL and social links (if any)
- High-resolution logo files; brand guidelines (if any)

**501(c)(3) track only**
- IRS 501(c)(3) determination letter
- Most recent Form 990
- Financial statements (past 2 years)
- Annual report and strategic plan (if available)

**Pre-501(c)(3) track instead**
- Articles of incorporation / state nonprofit formation documents (if filed)
- IRS Form 1023 status (filed, in progress, or planned)
- Charitable-solicitation registration status (if applicable)
- Planned board roster (names + planned roles)

## What happens after you submit

FFC works through four gates, in order. Each one must be complete before the next
starts — this protects volunteer time and means FFC only spends money on charities
that are clearly moving forward.

1. **Charity application approved.** FFC runs its validation checks: Candid status,
   your LinkedIn and Facebook Pages, board members' LinkedIn profiles, and more. On
   the 501(c)(3) track this includes a **Candid Gold seal** (Bronze → Silver → Gold;
   Gold needs your latest Form 990, and FFC also asks for your board roster and IRS
   letter on your Candid profile). Pre-501(c)(3) organizations defer the Candid
   steps until recognized.
2. **Website application approved** — a separate order in the same portal, which
   requires Gate 1.
3. **Website built and validated** on a free preview address like
   `https://freeforcharity.github.io/FFC-EX-yourcharity/` — no custom domain yet.
   You'll be added to its GitHub repository and asked to review and approve the
   content. (See `site-owner-setup.md`.)
4. **Domain** — only now does FFC register or transfer your domain in Cloudflare
   and point it at your validated site. **Email** (Microsoft 365 by default) comes
   after the domain, and is for recognized 501(c)(3)s.

FFC is volunteer-run and support is best-effort, so don't promise them a timeline.
If they've been waiting a long time, help them draft a polite status check with the
**ask-ffc-for-help** skill.

## Recommended alongside (501(c)(3) track)

These aren't application fields but FFC recommends them early because other tools
depend on them: **TechSoup** validation (guide: `techsoup`), **Goodstack**
verification — used by Google for Nonprofits and Canva (guide: `goodstack`), and an
organization profile on **Candid** (guide: `candid-organization`).
